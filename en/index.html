— Core / What sits at the center

From UX, down to the letter.

Turning what words miss into something you can touch.Translate the unspoken.

From designing how people will move, all the way down to a single letter of a logo or product name.
A single flyer or a whole website —I take it on as one continuous piece of work.
What we talk about first carries through to the very last letter, without being watered down along the way.

* For larger projects, I work with trusted partners.Even then, I remain your point of contact and own the overall design.

Four strengths.Range, continuity, results, handwork.Few people bring all four at once.

  • 01RangePrint, web and type.Even a name no typeface has, drawn letter by letter
  • 02ContinuityThe person you talk to is the person who makes it.Nothing gets lost in translation
  • 03ResultsAfter launch, I look at the numbers.Yataimura Matsuri — after the site renewal, bookings rose 120%
  • 04HandworkThere are hands in it.Brush, cut paper, paint. It doesn't all stay on the screen

Starting small is not about the budget —it's about commitment.Even one piece, once it is out in the world, carries your name.
When the contact and the maker are the same person, there is no one else to blame.

I'm based in Shiga,so I can also meet in person and look at the real thing together.

Kinoshita Studio observes experiences drawn from many fields — nature, the city, sound, the visual, the interface — and translates them into new experiences through design, sound and structure.
