— Behind the small request

Behind a request for “just one piece,”
there is usually a real
problem.

Most inquiries start with something small: one banner, one page, a touch-up to a logo. But as we talk, “what I really want is this” and “I’ve always wanted to do that” come up from underneath.

  • “We want a new look for our website.”

    Really: fewer customers were coming from search, and no one knew why.

  • “We want to redo our logo.”

    Really: every product looked different, so people couldn’t tell they came from the same company.

  • “We don’t have enough design hands.”

    Really: there were people to make things, but no one looking at the whole and making the calls.

So first, we put into words together the “this is what I want” behind the request.
A small request is fine. So is just a conversation.

— How We Start

STEP 01

First, let me make just one piece.

I don’t change everything at once. One title, one page, one label. Put out something small, and check first whether it works.

— Build big from the start, and there is no going back if it doesn’t work.

STEP 02

After it goes out, we look at the numbers together.

Launch is not the end. I watch only three things: inquiries, bookings, and what gets read. Watch more than that and you can’t act.

STEP 03

Then expand from what worked.

What worked is carried into the logo, packaging, website and signage. Only then does it become a full set.

— Do it in reverse and you get an impressive world that moves nothing.

Starting small is not about money.

It is fine if it ends with one piece. Decide whether to continue after you have seen it work.

But starting small is not about being cheap. It is about commitment.
Even one piece, once it is out in the world, carries your name.
So even for one piece, I take responsibility to the end.

When the contact and the maker are the same person, there is no one else to blame.

— Process

01 — Experience Research

Observe.

Observing and analyzing experience

Identify the gap between the experience a brand wants to deliver and the one customers actually feel. Through user research, field observation and brand analysis, make visible the problems no one had seen.

— Output

Experience Research Report
Gap mapping
Insight extraction

02 — Experience Strategy

Translate.

Designing the brand experience

Experience design that closes the gaps found in research. Bring together the brand concept, personas and customer journey, and put into words and structure the feeling that should be delivered.

— Output

Brand Experience Map
Persona / Journey
Experience Strategy

03 — Experience Design

Implement.

Implementing the experience

Turn the strategy into real UX and design. At every touchpoint — web, UI, visuals, content — shape things to deliver the experience the brand intends, and keep checking that the designed experience actually arrives.

— Output

UX / UI Design
Web / Visual Design
Design System
